## Figma AI Prompt

Create a premium, mobile-first **AI Sports Intelligence Platform** UI/UX for a direct-to-consumer football/soccer analytics subscription product.

The platform should feel like a high-end sports analytics brand, not a betting/tipster website. The design should communicate trust, intelligence, premium value, data accuracy, and responsible AI. The MVP includes a public website, subscriber dashboard, mobile/PWA views, Telegram integration flow, admin portal, pricing/subscription pages, and AI-powered prediction screens. The product starts with football/soccer and will later expand to other sports. 

### Brand Direction

Design style:

* Premium sports-tech
* Dark mode first
* Clean dashboard UI
* Data-rich but easy to understand
* Professional, trustworthy, modern
* Inspired by sports analytics, fintech dashboards, and AI intelligence platforms
* Avoid gambling-heavy visuals
* Use responsible prediction language: probability, confidence, risk level, model signal, data-driven insight

Suggested visual direction:

* Dark navy / black background
* Electric blue, green, and white accents
* Gold or premium accent for Elite membership
* Card-based layouts
* Modern charts and probability widgets
* Clean typography
* Rounded cards
* High contrast CTA buttons
* Mobile-first layouts

# Responsible Language Rules

Do not use:

* Guaranteed win
* Sure prediction
* 100% accurate
* Risk-free
* Bet now

Use instead:

* Model probability indicates higher likelihood
* High-confidence model signal
* Lower-risk according to current model factors
* Historically tracked accuracy
* Review the data-driven insight
